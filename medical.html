NanCal is not a medical device

Last updated: 2026-11-23

NanCal is a general wellness app for keeping a food and water diary. It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, monitor or prevent any disease or medical condition, and it does not give medical or dietary advice.

What the numbers are

  • Calorie and macro goals are estimates calculated from general formulas and the data you enter (sex, age, height, weight, activity, goal). Your real needs can differ.
  • AI estimates from photos, labels and descriptions are approximate and can be wrong, sometimes by a lot. Always check them before saving.
  • Food data from Open Food Facts and the community database is entered by volunteers and other users and may be incomplete, outdated or wrong. The app is not suitable for checking allergens.
  • Activity data (active calories, steps) comes from other apps and devices through Health Connect, and wearables often overestimate calories burned.

When to talk to a professional

Ask a doctor or registered dietitian before changing your diet or setting a weight goal, especially if you:

  • are pregnant or breastfeeding;
  • have diabetes, kidney, heart or other chronic disease, or take medication that depends on what you eat;
  • have or have had an eating disorder, or find that counting calories causes you distress;
  • are underweight or recovering from illness or surgery.

Do not use NanCal to calculate doses of insulin or any other medicine. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Age

NanCal is intended for adults aged 18 or over.
