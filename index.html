NanCal

A calorie and water tracker for Android and Wear OS. Your diary stays on your phone, and there is no account to create.

Get NanCal on Google Play

What it does

  • Food diary with calories and macros, favourites, recent foods and your own recipes.
  • Barcode scanning and search in the open Open Food Facts database, plus a built-in list of common foods.
  • AI estimates from a photo of your meal, a nutrition label or a short description. Every estimate is a suggestion that you can edit before it is saved.
  • Water tracking with optional reminders.
  • Health Connect (optional): active calories, steps and workouts from your other apps, and syncing water, weight and meals if you turn it on.
  • Wear OS app: today's status, a tile, watch face complications, and logging water or food from your wrist.
  • Your data, your files: backups to a place you choose and a CSV export.

What it is not

NanCal is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. Calorie goals, nutrition values and AI estimates are approximate and can be wrong. Read the full statement.

NanCal is intended for adults aged 18 or over.

Privacy in short

  • Your profile, diary, weight and water log are stored on your phone, not on our servers.
  • No ads, no advertising identifiers and no third-party analytics tools.
  • A photo or description leaves your phone only when you ask for an AI estimate.
  • An anonymous daily usage report is on by default and can be turned off in Settings.
