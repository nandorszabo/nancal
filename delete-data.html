Delete your data

Last updated: 2026-11-23

NanCal has no user accounts. Almost all of your data is on your phone, where you can delete it yourself at any time. A few technical records may exist on our server for your installation; you can delete those from the app, or ask us by e-mail.

1. Data on your phone

This is your profile, diary, weight, water log, custom foods, recipes, settings and saved AI keys.

  • Delete everything in the app: open Profile > About > Delete my data and choose "Delete everything". The app first asks our server to delete the records described in section 2, then deletes all of its data on the phone and restarts as if newly installed. Make a backup or an export first if you may need your data later.
  • Delete everything without the app: open Android Settings > Apps > NanCal > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the watch app removes its data from the watch.
  • Delete single items: delete diary entries, water, weight entries, foods and recipes inside the app.

Data stored outside the app is not removed by this:

  • Health Connect: delete what NanCal wrote there (water, weight, meals) in Health Connect: App permissions > NanCal > Delete app data, or Data and access.
  • Backup and export files: delete them where you saved them (for example in your files or your cloud storage).
  • Android backup: if your phone backs up app data to your Google account, manage or delete that backup in your Google account's backup settings.

2. Data on our server

Our server never holds your diary, profile, weight, photos or AI answers. If you used the built-in AI, a subscription or community foods, it may hold these records, stored under a hashed random identifier of your installation:

  • the installation record (app version, day first seen, day last seen);
  • the daily counter of built-in AI requests;
  • the link between your installation and a NanCal Pro subscription;
  • the daily counters of community food lookups, submissions and reports, and the marker that your installation confirmed or reported a community food.

Delete from the app (immediate)

In the app, open Profile > About > Delete my data, choose "Delete server data" and confirm. The app asks our server to delete every record listed above, and the server does so immediately. Do this before you clear the app's storage or uninstall it, because the request needs the installation identifier stored on your phone.

Request deletion by e-mail

Use this if you cannot use the app for it, for example because the deletion in the app fails on your device, or you prefer to ask us directly.

  1. In the app, open Profile > About > Delete my data and copy the installation reference shown there (64 letters and digits). We need it because we have no other way to tell which records belong to you.
  2. Send an e-mail to nancal.support@gmail.com with the subject "NanCal data deletion request" and paste the reference into the message. You do not need to give your name or a reason.
  3. We delete the records listed above and confirm by e-mail within 30 days. Your e-mail is used only to handle the request and is deleted once it is closed, unless the law requires us to keep it.

If you have already uninstalled the app or cleared its storage, the installation reference no longer exists. The remaining server records then cannot be connected to you, by us or by anyone else, and they are deleted automatically: the daily counters after 7 days, the marker next to community foods after 180 days, and the installation record and subscription link after 400 days without contact (the daily cleanup can take up to two more days). You can still write to us with any question.

What is kept after deletion

  • Anonymous usage counters: they are summed numbers without any identifier and cannot be traced back to an installation. You can stop future reports in Settings.
  • Community product facts (name, brand, nutrition values of a product you submitted) stay in the shared database, without any link to your installation. If a product you submitted is wrong, write to us and we will correct or remove it.
  • The subscription record itself (a hashed purchase token with its product, state and expiry) is kept while another installation uses the subscription; otherwise it is deleted 90 days after its last change. It is no longer linked to your installation.
  • Server logs: log lines of your earlier built-in AI requests and purchases contain the hashed installation identifier. They cannot be removed one by one and disappear within 30 days.

A subscription is not cancelled by deleting data. Cancel NanCal Pro in Google Play (Payments and subscriptions > Subscriptions). Purchase records held by Google belong to your Google account and are managed there.

3. Data held by others

Requests you sent with your own API key went directly to Google (Gemini) or Anthropic (Claude) and are handled under your own agreement with that provider. Searches and barcode lookups went to Open Food Facts. We hold no copy of these requests. For details see the Privacy Policy.
